StructureThree modes, one lifecycleThe editor already moves a change through draft, proposal and applied state. The layout follows that movement instead of holding one arrangement for all three: whichever panel you are actually working in gets the space, and the other two shrink rather than disappear.

Rules
A mode is offered, never forcedThe mode bar highlights the next mode when the state changes. You press it, or you do not; the segmented control is always visible and always live.
Nothing is hidden, only shrunkEvery panel exists in every mode. Chat becomes a 46 px rail in Refine, not a closed drawer, so its state stays readable.
Mode and density are separate axesMode decides which panel is primary. Density decides how much a panel holds. Neither has to know about the other.
